# NUC- ADRs that carry their clauses. The migration of the normalize skill applies it once with
# remap.sh, whose header gives the form of a line; the release notes point here.
#
# The six lines below are the map. An ADR whose clauses went to one NUC- ADR moves; one whose
# clauses went to several is a split, whose references remap.sh lists and leaves. Each split names
# every NUC- ADR that is the home of one of its clauses in the table after the map; a pointer in a
# home, such as an Out of scope that names another ADR, counts for nothing. Place each left
# reference by hand at the NUC- clause that carries what it cites, from that table.

# The clause table: each clause of an old ADR, shortened to a line, with its home. A row reads
# 'number | old clause | home'. In a home, bN is the Nth bullet of the clause; OoS is the Out of
# scope, Lat N the Nth item of the Latitude, Enf the Enforcement, and NMD its paragraph on what is
# not mechanically decidable. CM marks a clause whose meaning changed: a project ADR that rests on
# it is reviewed against its new home, and superseded where the change moves its basis.
